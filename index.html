I am a postdoctoral researcher at Åbo Akademi University in Turku, Finland. I earned my PhD in the Department of Pharmacy – Drug Sciences at the University of Bari Aldo Moro, and I hold a Master's degree in Chemistry, being a registered member of the Italian Order of Chemists. During my PhD I spent six months as a visiting researcher at Åbo Akademi, where I had previously carried out a three-month research stay during my bachelor's.

My research focuses on developing ultrasensitive biosensors, combining electronic transduction based on organic field-effect transistors (EGOFET) with optical techniques such as surface plasmon resonance (SPR), fluorescence, and UV–visible spectroscopy.

I also hold a second-level Master (MBA) in Economics and Management of Healthcare Organizations, with a thesis on data handling, privacy and blockchain carried out during an internship at ARESS (the Apulia regional health agency), in the Digital Transition and Privacy Office. The results of my work are collected in the publications listed below.
